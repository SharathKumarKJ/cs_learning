# Resume Tips for Data Engineers

1. Lead with impact, not tools. "Reduced ETL runtime 60% by repartitioning skewed joins" beats "Used PySpark".
2. Quantify: rows/day, GB, latency, cost saved, SLA improvement, users.
3. Highlight architecture: lakehouse, streaming, CDC, dimensional modeling.
4. Show ownership of production systems and on-call.
5. Include data quality and observability work.
6. Mention specific cloud services with versions when relevant.
7. Show breadth + one depth area (Spark, streaming, modeling, governance).
8. Avoid buzzwords without proof.
9. Keep to 1-2 pages even for senior roles.
10. Tailor per JD.

## Sample bullet templates
- Designed and implemented <pipeline> processing <volume> with <SLA>, using <stack>.
- Reduced <metric> by <X%> through <technique> (e.g. AQE, broadcast joins, partitioning).
- Migrated <system A> to <system B> resulting in <impact>.
- Led <team size> across <duration> delivering <outcome>.
